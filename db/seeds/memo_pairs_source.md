# memo pair seed source verification

Generation date: 2026-10-12. Generated by tools/memo_gen.py, committed output db/migrations/002_memo_pairs.sql.

## purpose

The BLD letter pair store maps every (orbit, pair) case, orbit 0 CT_ORBIT_CORNER and 1 CT_ORBIT_EDGE over all 576 two-letter Speffz combinations A through X, to a phoneme text and an image text. The phoneme text is the two letter atoms joined by one space and the image text is the two English words joined by one space, so every pair row is the composition of its two per-letter atoms and both orbits share the same atom set. Text only: no audio files, no TTS, no images on disk.

## pool construction rationale

The generator owns two hand-curated pools keyed by the 24 Speffz letters. The Vietnamese syllable pool holds 4 candidate syllables per letter, 96 entries total, each written by a Vietnamese speaker for this project and chosen so the letter's memo atom stays pronounceable in one breath: consonant-initial letters take syllables starting with a matching Vietnamese sound (B to b-, C/K to k-, D/GI to z-, PH to f-, QU to kw-, R to northern z-), vowel-initial letters (A, E, I, O, U) take vowel-initial syllables, and W takes the oa glide. Every syllable writing in the pool is distinct and every phoneme string in the pool is distinct, both asserted by the generator at startup. The English image pool holds 4 concrete memorable nouns per letter, each noun starting with the matching letter, all lowercase and distinct.

## phoneme notation definition

Each syllable entry carries a hand-written compact romanized-IPA phoneme string, no external G2P. Initials: b, k for c/k/q, kw for qu, z for the northern realizations of d, gi, and r, s for s and x, th, tr, ny for nh, ng, m, n, f for ph, v, l, h, g, and the empty onset for vowel-initial syllables. Rhymes: a, e, i, o, u, o^ for ô, e^ for ê, a^ for â, o~ for ơ, u~ for ư, glides w and j (ao to aw, ai to aj), ie for iê, uo for uô, uo~ for ươ, and finals m, n, ng (including final nh after a gliding vowel, anh to ajng), k for final c and ch, p, t. The tone rides as the final digit: 1 ngang, 2 huyền, 3 hỏi, 4 ngã, 5 sắc. The pool uses tones 1 through 5 only, no nặng syllables. Example: sách decomposes to s + aj + k + tone 5, phoneme string sajk5.

## evolution parameters and search

A deterministic evolutionary search picks one syllable atom and one image word per letter. Individual: a per-letter choice vector. Fitness, maximized lexicographically: first the minimum pairwise phoneme-string distance across the 24 chosen atoms, then the sum of pairwise distances as tiebreak. Distance: Levenshtein over the phoneme strings including the tone digit, normalized by the longer string. Population 64, generations 240, tournament selection of 3, elitism 2, uniform crossover over per-letter choices, mutation flipping 1 letter per child plus a second with probability 1 in 4, initial population seeded with one greedy min-distance individual plus random.Random(SEED) individuals, every random draw through that one generator instance.

Fixed seed: 20261012.

## achieved bound

Achieved minimum pairwise phoneme distance: 0.500000. Sum of pairwise distances: 248.000000. The generator prints the full 24 by 24 distance matrix on every run and asserts no two chosen atoms sit within a single edit of each other, which the 0.5 bound implies for these atom lengths. tests/unit_memo_seed.c re-derives the weaker direct bound from the committed data itself: 24 distinct atoms, pairwise Levenshtein at least 2, atoms consistent across both orbits.
