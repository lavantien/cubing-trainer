# provenance additions drafted for phase 2, to be merged into db/seeds/packs/PROVENANCE.md

These two sections extend the existing bld_oldpoch.tsv and bld_m2.tsv sections. Parity, buffer edge flip, and buffer corner twist cases are outside the 3-style generator's reach by construction, commutators of face turns are even permutations with constrained orientation sums, so they ship as curated rows. Landing sequence per the lead: commit 1 is this file's method-pack rows, commit 2 is the ref-sheet special-category rows built from playground/ref_ground/ validated TSVs, commit 3 is the enumeration and selector wiring. The blddb reference sheets in ref/ are the primary source for the app's special categories, this file's web-sourced rows are the Old Pochmann and M2 method-pack layer plus corroboration.

## bld_oldpoch.tsv parity and corner twist rows

Sources: op.08 parity reverified against https://jperm.net/bld fetched live again this pass, capture at playground/parity_flip_twist/jperm_bld.md, and against J Perm's tutorial description at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A64Sy4WKiWY, capture at playground/parity_flip_twist/jperm_op_video.md. op.39 corner twist sourced from http://www.stefan-pochmann.de/spocc/blindsolving/M2R2/, capture at playground/parity_flip_twist/pochmann_m2r2.md. op.40 parity companion sourced from https://bldtrainer.com/parity, capture at playground/parity_flip_twist/bldtrainer.md. Retrieved 2026-10-10.

Page contents: J Perm's page prints the parity algorithm R U' R' U' R U R D R' U' R D' R' U2 R' U' verbatim, unchanged from the m5 capture, with the usage claim to apply it between solving corners and edges when both letter counts are odd. His description FAQ states flipped and twisted pieces are handled as new cycles in Old Pochmann, no dedicated algorithm exists on any Old Pochmann origin checked: his page, his description, the speedsolving wiki Old Pochmann page, and bldtrainer.com all document cycle break handling only, captures in playground/parity_flip_twist/. Pochmann's M2/R2 page carries the orient corners list, its third entry is the twist row of record. bldtrainer's parity lesson publishes the parity companion R U R' F' R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' with the effect claim "it swaps the leftover two edges and two corners (no centers move)", and bldtrainer's edge buffer UF and corner buffer UBL match the shipped letter table.

Case key derivation: op.08 already shipped, op.39 is the corner twist row, op.40 is the bldtrainer parity companion. front_json records the kind and the simulated effect, op.39 twists UBR and UFR in opposite directions with zero permutation, op.40 swaps UF-UR plus UFR-UBR.

Decision recorded: op.39 lands verbatim with no conjugation. No sourced algorithm twists the buffer corner directly, Pochmann's entries 1 and 2 twist UBR and DFR, the R2 method's own misoriented buffers per his text, entries 3 and 4 twist the U layer pair UBR and UFR in opposite directions, so entry 3 is stored as the buffer agnostic twist algorithm and its front_json states what it does rather than presenting a derived conjugation as sourced text.

Corroboration recorded for op.40: the alg is byte-identical to Tommy Cherry's parity sheet row "UF-UR, UFR-UBR" in ref/Tommy Cherry's 3BLD algs.xlsx, verified by direct sheet extraction this pass, and that row's stated positions equal the simulated effect. Community tutorial text equals the world class solver's reconstructed execution, two independent chains.

Validation: playground/parity_flip_twist/parity_flip_probe.c simulates every row through the real engine, output in sim_output.txt. op.08 swaps UB with UL and UBR with UFR, all oriented, centers fixed, odd permutation in both orbits, and replays J Perm's example solve 2 end to end to a solved cube, scramble plus 13 edge swaps plus parity plus 9 corner swaps. op.39 twists UBR and UFR in place with zero permutation, edges and centers restored. op.40 swaps UF with UR sticker exact plus UBR with UFR, centers fixed, exactly the bldtrainer claim and exactly the ref sheet position label. All row texts parse grammar clean under ct_notation_parse at n=3 with no bracket stripping, the twist row stores the source tokens with the grouping parentheses dropped, matching the existing probe normalization precedent.

## bld_m2.tsv parity and buffer flip rows

Sources: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JTkSQxWk_u8, J Perm's M2 tutorial description, capture at playground/parity_flip_twist/jperm_m2_video.md. http://www.cubefreak.net/bld/m2_guide.html, capture at playground/parity_flip_twist/cubefreak_m2.md. http://www.stefan-pochmann.de/spocc/blindsolving/M2R2/, capture at playground/parity_flip_twist/pochmann_m2r2.md. The speedsolving wiki M2 page was refetched live, capture at playground/parity_flip_twist/speedsolving_m2.md, and documents no parity companion and no buffer flip case, which is why these rows source elsewhere. Retrieved 2026-10-10.

Page contents: J Perm's M2 description lists two parity algorithms, option 1 M2 U' M U2 M U' M' U2 M' U2 and option 2 D' L2 D M2 D' L2 D, the first listed option is the row of record per the pack convention, with the usage claim to apply one after solving edges and before solving corners on an odd letter count, and his transcript states the effect: the M slice is off by one after odd edges, the parity algorithm leaves all edges solved except the two that Old Pochmann corner solving then swaps an odd number of times. Pochmann's page lists M' U M' U M' U2 M U M U M U2 under orient edges. cubefreak's guide states the M2 endgame rule, orient the flipped inactive edges and orient the DF edge in addition when their count is odd, and its example 2 applies F2 M' U M' U M' U2 M U M U M U2 F2 stating it leaves the (DF DR) 2-cycle with correct orientation.

Case key derivation: m2.23 and m2.24 follow the shipped m2.01 to m2.22. front_json carries the kind, m2.24 carries the pack's documented DF buffer and the flipped pair.

Normalization: m2.23 stored verbatim as printed. m2.24's source prints the step with hyphen separators and compact runs, F2-M'UM'UM'U2MUMUMU2-F2, stored expanded to spaced tokens, F2 M' U M' U M' U2 M U M U M U2 F2, tokens untouched. Both parse grammar clean under ct_notation_parse at n=3.

Validation: playground/parity_flip_twist/parity_flip_probe.c, output in sim_output.txt. Both parity options share one effect and replay J Perm's M2 example solve 1 end to end, scramble plus 11 targets plus parity leaves the edges solved except exactly the (UL UB) pair, which equals op.02's simulated edge side effect, corners untouched by the edge phase, centers solved. The flip base algorithm purely flips UB and UF, its F2 conjugate flips UB and DF, the DF buffer itself, both with zero permutation and centers restored. cubefreak example 1 replays to fully solved edges and centers. cubefreak example 2 replays to exactly the claimed (DF DR) 2-cycle with no flipped edges remaining.

Source erratum, recorded not silently fixed in the artifact: cubefreak example 2's printed pair 4 uses the setup BL'B' where the page's own preparation table defines LD as BLB', and BL'B' is the table's LU. The simulation confirms the printed form cycles the UL piece instead of DL and misses the claimed end state. The replay uses the table corrected form BLB' with the erratum printed by the probe. The shipped rows are unaffected, the erratum concerns only the example replay evidence.

Enum convention: unchanged from the existing sections, CT_EVENT_3BLD, CT_METHOD_M2 with CT_STAGE_EP and CT_METHOD_OLD_POCHMANN with CT_STAGE_PLL at load time.
