Clock methods are methods for solving the Rubik's Clock. They can be divided into 2 groups:

  • Flip methods: they require to flip the clock at some point of the solve. These methods are generally considered to be easier since most of the moves are intuitive and easy to understand but they are generally not the best for Speedsolving since, at these methods, both sides of the clock are solved one separately which is might be inefficient.
  • No-Flip methods: these methods uses memorization techniques and Simul moves to solve both sides of the clock without flipping it. These methods has generally a lower move count which is considered better in competitive scenario although they're mostly harder to learn since it requires to be able to do memorization during the 15 seconds inspection and to get used with simul moves which can be hard at first.
