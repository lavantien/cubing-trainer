## the reversed fix

The classifier orientation gate from the ref-compare cross-check, 3-cycle sticker shape passing for twisted and flipped rows, was initially taken as a defect and fixed in code, then reversed. The decomposition boundary is sticker-level by design, matching the fixture oracle, and the structure recall question on conjugated human forms is a labeling decision rather than a defect. The reversal landed as dc842fc, a docs-only pin of the classification boundary at sticker level with the fixture oracle, and the classifier is accepted as designed.
